## New

- Install hlabs on a Mac from its disk image. It offers to move itself to Applications, lives in the menu bar and sets up a container engine if your Mac doesn't have one.
- Install hlabs on a Linux server with one command: `curl -fsSL https://hlabs.h4loom.com/install.sh | sh`. It checks the computer, installs Docker if it's missing, checks every download against its signature and tells you where to finish setup.
- A Linux desktop app as a `.deb`, `.rpm` or AppImage, with hlabs in your system tray.
- Uninstall hlabs from the menu-bar app on a Mac, or with `sudo hlabs uninstall` on a Linux server, keeping your data or deleting everything.
- The `hlabs` command on a Linux server: `hlabs status`, `hlabs logs`, `hlabs setup-url` and `hlabs reset-password`.
- Everything from before this release: apps in one click from the App Store, Home and the Dock, Files, backups to a NAS, a drive or the cloud, remote access with Tailscale, accounts for the family, live usage and updates.
- A website with downloads, every app hlabs can install, release notes and the help. When hlabs shows an error, **Learn more** opens its page there.

## Before you update

This is a beta: it may have bugs, so keep a backup of anything you can't lose. The Mac version isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS asks first: choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
