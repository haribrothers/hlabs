For everyone who uses hlabs: the person who set it up, and the family and friends they invited.

hlabs runs apps like photo libraries, media servers and password managers on a Mac or Linux computer at home, with your files, backups and access from anywhere in one dashboard. Pages for things only admins can do say so at the top.

## Start here

- **New to hlabs?** [Install it on a Mac](install/mac/), [on a Linux server](install/linux-server/) or [on a Linux desktop](install/linux-desktop/), then [set it up](get-started/set-up/).
- **Invited by someone?** [Log in](get-started/log-in/) with the link they sent, then open your apps from [Home](get-started/home/).
