For everyone who uses hlabs.

**Settings › Appearance** changes how hlabs looks for you. Every change shows straight away and is saved to your account, so it follows you to every device you log in on. Other people on this hlabs keep their own look.

## Wallpaper

Choose **Dusk** (the one everyone starts with), **Ocean** or **Forest**. The wallpaper behind every window changes as soon as you click it.

To use a photo of your own, choose **+ Upload photo** and pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP file of up to 10 MB. It appears as **Your own** and becomes your wallpaper. hlabs keeps one photo per person, shrunk to at most 3840 pixels wide or tall, and removes the details cameras store in photos (such as where it was taken). Only you can see it. Uploading another photo replaces it, and you can switch back to it after choosing another wallpaper.

If the file is too big or isn't one of those kinds of picture, hlabs says "Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP under 10 MB" and your wallpaper stays as it was.

## Accent colour

Pick **Violet**, **Mint**, **Amber** or **Rose**. Buttons, switches, selected items and the outline that shows where you are with the keyboard all change colour at once. With a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move between the colours.

## Home screen

- **Show widgets**: turn it off and Home shows only your apps, without the row of widgets.
- **Show greeting**: turn it off and Home leaves out "Good morning", "Good afternoon" or "Good evening" and your name.

Both are on to begin with.

## Easier to read and calmer

- **Reduce transparency** swaps the see-through glass for solid backgrounds, which are easier to read.
- **Reduce motion** turns off the icon bounce, window animations and moving highlights; things appear and fade instead.

If your device is set to reduce motion (in its accessibility settings), hlabs starts with **Reduce motion** on. Once you change the switch here, your choice wins on every device.
