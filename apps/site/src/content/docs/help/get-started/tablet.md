For everyone who uses hlabs.

On an iPad or another tablet, hlabs looks the way it does on a computer, with the **Dock** at the bottom and areas such as **Files** and **Settings** in windows, and it works by touch. Buttons, rows, switches and tabs are big enough for a finger, and nothing needs a mouse. If you connect a mouse or trackpad, hlabs switches to the computer's layout straight away, without reloading. On a phone, hlabs has a layout of its own (see [Use hlabs on your phone](../phone/)).

## Touch and hold

Touch and hold something for half a second to open the menu a right-click opens on a computer. The tablet vibrates once if it can.

- **An app on Home**: the app lifts over a blurred Home with its menu beside it: **Open**, **Settings**, **View logs**, **Restart**, **Stop** (or **Start**), **Uninstall…** and **Edit Home**. People who aren't admins see **Open** and **Edit Home**.
- **An app pinned to the Dock**: **Open**, **Open in a new tab**, **App settings** (admins) and **Remove from Dock**.
- **A file or folder in Files**: the same menu as a right-click, at your finger. To select several items, tap **Select** at the top.
- **A drive in Files**: **Disconnect**.
- **A chart**: hold your finger on it to see the value there, and slide along to see others.

Tap anywhere outside a menu to close it. That tap only closes the menu; it doesn't open whatever is under your finger. If your finger moves before the half second is up, hlabs scrolls the page instead.

Holding the empty part of Home starts **Edit Home**, and on a tablet there's also an **Edit Home** button next to the bell at the top right.

## Drag with your finger

Touch and hold, then move your finger without lifting it:

- **Home, in Edit Home**: the app or widget follows your finger and goes where you lift it. The page stays still while you drag. Tap **Done** to keep the new order.
- **The Dock**: move a pinned app sideways along the Dock to put it somewhere else.
- **Files**: move a file or folder over another folder. **Move to Taxes** shows as you reach it; lift your finger to move it there, then tap **Undo** in the message if you didn't mean to. Lift it anywhere else and nothing moves.

## The Dock

Tap a tile to open it straight away. Tiles don't grow under your finger; to see a tile's name, touch and hold it. The Dock sits above the bar at the bottom of the screen that takes you to the iPad's Home Screen.

## iPad held upright

In portrait, windows take the full width of the screen. **Settings**, **Files** and the **App Store** start with a list instead of a sidebar: tap a section, place or category to go into it, and **‹ Settings**, **‹ Files** or **‹ App Store** at the top to come back. Home shows four apps to a row and two widgets to a row. When you turn the iPad, hlabs changes its layout at once and stays on the page you're on.

## Typing

When the on-screen keyboard opens, the field you're typing in moves up above it, and a window such as **New folder** stays in the part of the screen you can see. The Dock steps aside while the keyboard is open.

## Add hlabs to the iPad's Home Screen

In Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. hlabs then opens without Safari's bars, with the Dock above the bottom of the screen and nothing under the status bar at the top. See [Use hlabs as an app](../use-as-app/).
