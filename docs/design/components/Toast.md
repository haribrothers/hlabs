# Toast

A short notice that something finished or went wrong, shown top-right for about five seconds.

Level-3 glass, radius `radius-lg`, `shadow-menu`. Tone icon (neutral `Info`, success `CircleCheck`, warning `TriangleAlert`, danger `CircleX`) in `ink-muted`, `success`, `warning` or `danger`; title `body-sm` at 600, text `caption`. Danger toasts stay until dismissed and use `role="alert"`.

One line of title, one of detail, at most one action ("View log", "Undo").
